One-button network check

How is your connection?

Check the network you are on, then share a result with your helpdesk if you choose.

Run this on your own device, not inside the remote desktop.

Choose what to check

This fork checks custom hosts. A shared result may name your organization; review it before sharing publicly.

All geographies start selected. A selected target region is included even if its geography is unchecked.

What this page contacts

The page itself and the regional endpoints listed for the selected pack. The AVD pack also checks the listed AVD end-user hosts. Each contacted service receives the public IP address at your network's edge (which may be a proxy or VPN) and your browser user-agent, as with any visit. The result file is not uploaded.

The full check downloads an 8 MiB file from this site. Add ?lite to the link to skip that download.

          What a browser can and cannot tell you

          This check measures HTTPS requests from this browser. It does not test sign-in, your access to a desktop, wildcard endpoints, UDP or the session host. A proxy or content blocker may affect the result. Regional timings are to a service endpoint, not to your desktop host.
